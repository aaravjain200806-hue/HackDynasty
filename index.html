The Inception of HackDynasty

Founded on September 15, 2025, by Aarav Jain, Naitik Jain, Kanishak Jain, and Arhan Jain, HackDynasty is a competitive hackathon team dedicated to rapid engineering and technical innovation. We thrive under intense 24-to-48-hour sprints—moving beyond simple proof-of-concepts to design, build, and deploy clean, production-ready software that solves real problems under the clock.

Core Drive: High velocity. Clean architecture. Working software delivered under pressure.
